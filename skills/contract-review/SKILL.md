# Contract Review

Review the named design or packet against its acceptance criteria, scope,
artifacts, security controls, and compatibility boundaries.

- Inspect the exact revision and artifact hashes.
- Record accepted, rejected, or pending outcomes in the governed review record.
- Report concrete findings with evidence and required fixes.
- Never approve your own work or change approval records as a side effect.
- A review recommendation is not user approval and does not admit a packet.
